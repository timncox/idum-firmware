Beatgen Programmer

Edit the five drum patterns inside an IDUM Beatgen firmware build, hear them, and flash them to the module from Chrome.

1. Load the firmware you built

Pick the alt-dum-beatgen .hex from make beatgen-build. Your patterns are read straight out of it.

2. Edit patterns

rest 1 rarely 2 sometimes 3 often X always Click to raise a step, shift-click or right-click to clear.

Faded steps don't play at this PROB setting. PARAM on the module adds fills and ghost notes on top.

3. Flash

  1. Plug the module's Nano Every into this computer over USB (Eurorack power can stay on).
  2. Press Flash and pick the Nano Every in Chrome's port list. It erases and rewrites the firmware, then reads it back to check.
  3. Flip CYCLE up to hear Beatgen; down is the stock IDUM engine.

Beatgen runs on Alt-DUM (github.com/ctanis/idum-firmware), alternate firmware based on IDUM v0.99 by Eli Pechman / Mystic Circuits. The browser flasher is from ID00M by 00 Modular. All CC BY-SA 4.0.
